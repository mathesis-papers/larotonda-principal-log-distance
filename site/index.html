Trace-coupled counterexamples to Larotonda's principal-log distance conjecture: beyond the Antezana-Larotonda-Varela theorem

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceCitationRepositoryMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

Beyond the unitarily invariant theorem of Antezana, Larotonda and Varela (2011/2014; Theorem 4.3 of arXiv:1107.2439v1), Larotonda's conjecture after Proposition 4.3 in Section 4.1, p. 7 of Short curves in homogeneous spaces of the unitary group for unitarily invariant norms (arXiv:2610.31535v1) fails for a normalized, symmetric Ad-invariant quadratic norm on u(2): shifting one eigenangle by 2 pi preserves the endpoint and gives a shorter exponential curve. For the full positive-definite family obtained by adding c times the squared trace to the Hilbert-Schmidt norm squared, we show in every dimension at least two that principal-log minimality holds for all principal angles if and only if the trace coefficient is zero. Scalar examples give a strict failure whenever their eigenangle exceeds pi(1+c)/(1+nc), for c>0. Thus the proposed pi/2 input already fails in dimension three, and no positive dimension-independent spectral radius suffices.
